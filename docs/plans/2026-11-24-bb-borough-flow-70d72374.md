# Plan: Borough-to-borough flow, Q1 2024

**Goal:** Publish `agg_borough_flow_monthly`, the complete Q1 2024 borough-to-borough table with its trip count, revenue, cross-borough share and unresolved flags, as a contracted boundary relation with its semantic-layer source and the tests that prove it.

**Requirement:** [`docs/requirement/2026-11-24-bb-borough-flow-70d72374.md`](../requirement/2026-11-24-bb-borough-flow-70d72374.md)

**Constraints:**

- Grain: one row per (`month_start_date`, `origin_borough`, `dest_borough`); the grid is complete at 8 boroughs × 8 boroughs × 3 months = 192 rows, and a pair-month with no billable trip publishes as a row of zero trips and zero revenue ([ADR 0018](../adr/0018-borough-flows-carry-no-volume-floor.md)).
- Population: `fct_trip` trips with `is_billable` true whose `pickup_datetime` falls in the reporting window, taken through the `in_report_window` macro so the dates never leave `dbt_project.yml` ([ADR 0003](../adr/0003-billable-trip-definition-and-where-it-applies.md)).
- Both zone roles come from `dim_zone` joined on the trip's own `pickup_zone_key` / `dropoff_zone_key` — the version in force on the trip's date, never the current version ([ADR 0009](../adr/0009-zone-attributes-are-as-of-the-trip-date.md), design `D-03`).
- Unresolved zones are real dimension rows and stay in the table as their own group, flagged per axis from `dim_zone.is_unresolved_zone` ([ADR 0012](../adr/0012-unresolved-zones-are-retained-and-flagged.md), [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-quality-verdicts-flag-trips-and-never-delete-them.md), design `D-04`).
- `is_cross_borough` is true only when both ends resolve to a borough and the two differ ([ADR 0019](../adr/0019-cross-borough-trips-exclude-unresolved-zones.md), design `D-07`).
- `cross_borough_share_pct` is `100 * cross_borough_trip_count / resolved_trip_count` through `safe_divide` ([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-rates-are-rebuilt-from-summed-numerators-and-denominators.md)); `cross_borough_trip_count` and `resolved_trip_count` are both-ends-resolved counts of the origin-month, repeated on every row of that origin (design `D-06`).
- The share is null on every row whose origin is unresolved and on every row whose destination is unresolved (`R-04`, `R-05`); the two counts stay published on those rows so a reader sees the basis. On a row where both ends are resolved it equals the origin-month value exactly.
- No volume floor: a pair below 25 trips is published, and `sum(trip_count)` over the whole table equals the window's billable trips (`R-06`, ADR 0018).
- Output columns are fixed by design `D-10`: `month_start_date` DATE, `month_name` VARCHAR, `calendar_year` INTEGER, `origin_borough` VARCHAR, `origin_is_unresolved` BOOLEAN, `dest_borough` VARCHAR, `dest_is_unresolved` BOOLEAN, `is_cross_borough` BOOLEAN, `trip_count` BIGINT, `total_revenue` DOUBLE, `resolved_trip_count` BIGINT, `cross_borough_trip_count` BIGINT, `cross_borough_share_pct` DOUBLE. Every output expression is cast to its declared type (DuckDB does not coerce to a contract, design `D-08`).
- Materialisation: table, with `contract: {enforced: true}` in `transformation/models/marts/agg/_agg.yml`, like every other boundary model in `agg`.
- Every dbt command runs in the Intent sandbox through the runtime wrapper from `transformation/`: `/data/default-plugins/vibedata-data-engineering/0.148.26/scripts/vd-intent-context-run.sh "dbt <command> --target dev"`. No production manifest is published for `duckdb_local`, so no `--defer` and nothing under `/data/cache`.

### Task 2: The singular tests that prove the promises

**Requirements:** `R-01`–`R-08` · **Design:** [`agg_borough_flow_monthly.md`](../design/models/agg/agg_borough_flow_monthly.md) · **Skill:** `dbt-testing` · **Depends on:** Task 1

- [ ] Author these seven tests under `transformation/tests/`, each returning the rows that violate its assertion and carrying a one-line comment naming the rule it holds:
  - `assert_borough_flow_grain_is_unique.sql` — `R-01`: no (`month_start_date`, `origin_borough`, `dest_borough`) repeats.
  - `assert_borough_flow_grid_is_complete.sql` — `R-02`: every month of `dim_date` in the report window crossed with every `dim_zone` borough, both as origin and as destination, is present; a missing cell is a row of the test.
  - `assert_borough_flow_cells_match_fct_trip.sql` — `R-03`, `R-07`: an independent recomputation of `count(*)` and `sum(total_amount)` from `fct_trip` joined twice to `dim_zone` over billable in-window trips, full-outer-joined to the relation, differs in no cell; revenue compared at the cent and zero cells included.
  - `assert_borough_flow_reconciles_to_fct_trip.sql` — `R-06`, `R-08`: `sum(trip_count)` equals the window's billable trips and `round(sum(total_revenue), 2)` equals its billable revenue, compared with `is distinct from`; a second check returns a row when no pair between 1 and 24 trips is published, which is what a volume floor would remove.
  - `assert_borough_flow_share_matches_its_counts.sql` — `R-04`: on every row where both ends are resolved, the share equals `100 * cross_borough_trip_count / resolved_trip_count` within `1e-6` relative tolerance, and it is not null.
  - `assert_borough_flow_unresolved_cells_carry_no_share.sql` — `R-05`: any row with `origin_is_unresolved` or `dest_is_unresolved` that carries a non-null `cross_borough_share_pct` is a violation, and the rows are still published with their `trip_count`.
  - `assert_borough_flow_flags_match_dim_zone.sql` — `D-04`, `D-07`: `origin_is_unresolved` / `dest_is_unresolved` differ from `dim_zone.is_unresolved_zone` for that borough on no row, and `is_cross_borough` differs from "both resolved and different" on no row.
- [ ] Add the seven filenames to `test-map.json` `modules.agg.data` and add `transformation/models/marts/agg/_unit_tests.yml` to `modules.agg.unit` with `modules.agg.selectors.unit` = `path:models/marts/agg` and `modules.agg.commands.unit` = `cd transformation && dbt test --select path:models/marts/agg,test_type:unit`; leave `modules.agg.commands.data` unchanged (edit with a Python load/dump; the file is large).
- [ ] Author the unit test `borough_flow_keeps_zero_cells_and_blanks_the_share_on_an_unresolved_end` in `transformation/models/marts/agg/_unit_tests.yml`, on the same pattern as `transformation/models/intermediate/_unit_tests.yml`: fixtures for `ref('dim_date')` (one month, 2024-01-01), `ref('dim_zone')` (one resolved zone in Manhattan, one in Brooklyn, one unresolved zone in Unknown) and `ref('fct_trip')` (one Manhattan→Brooklyn billable trip, one Manhattan→Unknown billable trip, one non-billable trip), with `overrides: {vars: {report_start: '2024-01-01', report_end: '2024-04-01'}}`; expect three rows, `Manhattan/Manhattan` at zero trips and a null share, `Manhattan/Brooklyn` at one trip and share 100.0, `Manhattan/Unknown` at one trip and a null share.
