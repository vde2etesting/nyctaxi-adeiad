- Q1 billable population with both zones resolved: 9,604,511 trips and 263,480,834.99 revenue. — `with t as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime) as mth, o.borough_name as origin, dz.borough_name as dest, f.total_amount from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key = o.zone_key join core.dim_zone dz on f.dropoff_zone_key = dz.zone_key where f.is_billable and f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01') select count(*) as billable_trips, round(sum(total_amount),2) as revenue from t`
