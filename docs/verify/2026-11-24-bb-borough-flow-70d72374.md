## Discovery

- **Input adequacy: `Ready`.** `core.fct_trip` in this Intent's sandbox (`target: ephemeral`), located because the Design's `## Consumers` and the Requirement name it and its schema resolves inside the sandbox copy. 9,722,336 trips picked up in 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31, of which 9,604,511 are billable, summing to 263,480,834.99; 0 null `pickup_zone_key`, 0 null `dropoff_zone_key`, 0 billable trips with a null `total_amount`; pickups span 2024-01-01T00:00:00 to 2024-03-31T23:59:59. — `select count(*) as in_window_trips, count(*) filter (where is_billable) as billable, count(*) filter (where pickup_zone_key is null) as null_pickup_zone_key, count(*) filter (where dropoff_zone_key is null) as null_dropoff_zone_key, count(*) filter (where is_billable and total_amount is null) as billable_null_total, round(sum(total_amount) filter (where is_billable), 2) as billable_revenue, min(pickup_datetime) as first_pickup, max(pickup_datetime) as last_pickup from core.fct_trip where pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01'`
- **Input adequacy: `Ready`.** `core.dim_zone`, same sandbox: 272 rows, 272 distinct `zone_key`, 265 current versions, 2 unresolved zones, 0 null `borough_name`, 8 distinct borough values. — `select count(*) as dim_rows, count(*) filter (where is_current_version) as current_rows, count(*) filter (where borough_name is null) as null_borough, count(*) filter (where is_unresolved_zone) as unresolved_rows, count(distinct zone_key) as distinct_zone_key, count(distinct borough_name) as distinct_borough from core.dim_zone`
- **The eight borough values**, from the Domain warehouse (`target: domain`) when the Requirement was written: Bronx, Brooklyn, EWR, Manhattan, N/A, Queens, Staten Island, Unknown; N/A and Unknown are the two flagged unresolved zones (ADR 0012). — `select borough_name, count(*) as zones, sum(case when is_unresolved_zone then 1 else 0 end) as unresolved from core.dim_zone where is_current_version group by 1 order by 1`
- **Edge periods are not cut short.** Pickups in the window cover 31 days of January, 29 of February (2024 is a leap year) and 31 of March, with the window's first pickup on 2024-01-01 and last on 2024-03-31. — `select date_trunc('month', pickup_datetime) as m, count(*) as trips, count(distinct pickup_date) as days, min(pickup_date) as first_day, max(pickup_date) as last_day from core.fct_trip group by 1 order by 1`
- **Pair-months with no trip: 12.** A full 3 × 8 × 8 grid minus the observed pairs leaves 12 absent cells: EWR→Bronx and EWR→Brooklyn in all three months, Bronx→Staten Island in February and March, Staten Island→Bronx in February and March, Unknown→Staten Island in January, Bronx→EWR and Staten Island→EWR in March. — `with b as (select distinct borough_name from core.dim_zone), m as (select distinct date_trunc('month', pickup_datetime)::date as month_start_date from core.fct_trip where pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01'), t as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime)::date as m, o.borough_name ob, d.borough_name db, count(*) n from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key=o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key=d.zone_key where f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01' and f.is_billable group by 1,2,3) select m.month_start_date, b1.borough_name as origin_borough, b2.borough_name as dest_borough from m cross join b b1 cross join b b2 left join t on t.m=m.month_start_date and t.ob=b1.borough_name and t.db=b2.borough_name where t.n is null order by 1,2,3`
- **A floor would have dropped real volume.** Under a 25-billable-trip floor, 17, 18 and 14 of the 61, 60 and 59 observed pair-months would disappear, taking 93, 117 and 113 trips with them — the evidence behind the no-floor answer (ADR 0018). — `with pairs as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime)::date as month_start_date, o.borough_name as origin_borough, d.borough_name as dest_borough, count(*) as trip_count, sum(f.total_amount) as revenue from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key = o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key = d.zone_key where f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01' and f.is_billable group by 1,2,3) select month_start_date, count(*) as pairs, count(*) filter (where trip_count < 25) as pairs_under_25, sum(trip_count) filter (where trip_count < 25) as trips_under_25, sum(trip_count) as trips_total from pairs group by 1 order by 1`
- **The cross-borough share's two candidate definitions differ materially.** Per origin and month, counting an unresolved destination as a crossing gives Manhattan 5.83% in January against 5.45% on resolved destinations, and Queens 73.14% against 72.29%; the answers chose the resolved-destination basis (ADR 0019), under which the share equals the off-diagonal resolved cells over the resolved cells of that origin. — `with t as (select date_trunc('month', f.pickup_datetime)::date as m, o.borough_name as ob, d.borough_name as db, count(*) as n from core.fct_trip f join core.dim_zone o on f.pickup_zone_key = o.zone_key join core.dim_zone d on f.dropoff_zone_key = d.zone_key where f.pickup_datetime >= timestamp '2024-01-01' and f.pickup_datetime < timestamp '2024-04-01' and f.is_billable group by 1,2,3), resolved as (select * from t where ob not in ('N/A','Unknown') and db not in ('N/A','Unknown')) select r.m, r.ob as origin_borough, sum(r.n) as resolved_trips, sum(case when r.db <> r.ob then r.n else 0 end) as cross_trips, round(100.0 * sum(case when r.db <> r.ob then r.n else 0 end) / sum(r.n), 2) as cross_share_pct, (select sum(n) from t where t.m = r.m and t.ob = r.ob) as all_origin_trips from resolved r group by 1,2 order by 1,2`
- **Not probed, and why.** No other relation is read by this model: the borough flow reads `fct_trip` and `dim_zone` only, so no third profile was needed. The zone-lookup borough values were profiled on the Domain target rather than the sandbox at Requirement time because no model was under construction yet; the sandbox copy reports the same 8 values and the same 2 unresolved zones.
